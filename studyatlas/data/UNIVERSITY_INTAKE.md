# University data intake (Ver.1.7.2)

_Generated by `npm run universities` from `data/schools/universities.ts`, `data/schools/universityPrograms.ts` and the access guard. Do not edit by hand. Nothing here requests a site._

## What is and is not in the data

- **Recorded** for all 13: name, city, link to the university's own home page (unverified, link not opened), and an *editorial* formal name and main campuses (unverified: general knowledge, not matched to an official source).
- **Not recorded** (pending): the six official pages (programme search, international admissions, tuition, deadlines, English requirements, scholarships) and every programme. No fee, requirement or deadline exists in the data: nothing is estimated, and a guessed link is not entered because it would not be a source.
- A programme is compared only when it has a recorded level and its facts have a source, a year, a currency and a billing unit; otherwise the university stays "coming soon".

## Why nothing was collected

Every university host below is **unconfirmed** for the access guard (no rights record, no human review): the collector may not request it, and robots.txt does not change that. A rights record or a human review opens a host only for the ways and URLs a person has read in the terms (`npm run review -- template <host>`; procedure in `collector/README.md`). Until then a person can supply the official page text or a permitted dataset, and fill a record by hand.

## How to add a programme (a person does this)

1. Open the university's official programme page in a browser and read it (human reading is not automated access).
2. Add a record to `UNIVERSITY_PROGRAMS["<id>"]` in `data/schools/universityPrograms.ts`: `id`, `degree`, `major`, `level` (bachelor / master / …), and for every field you can confirm a Fact with `sources` (https + publisher), `period` (the academic year), `checkedAt` (the day you read it). Tuition needs `amount`, ISO `currency`, `unit` (year / term / course …), `feeYear`, `applicability` (who it applies to) and `tax`.
3. Leave anything you could not confirm out, or `pending`. Never enter an estimate. Entry requirements and deadlines belong to the programme, not to the university.
4. A fact is `verified` only with an entry in `PROGRAM_APPROVALS` (who, when, what was compared). `npm test` and the build refuse an incomplete record.
5. Run `npm run universities`.
